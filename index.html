Heat risk and distance weighted heat risk for population over 65 in SW Finland

About this map

This webmap visualises the heat risk faced by population aged over 65 in Southwest Finland, and reclassified weighted risk layers calculated based on distanes to the nearest hospitals offering emergency services. Hospitals and health centres are also visualised on the map. The map is aimed at healthcare actors who are planning healthcare services.

Distance classes:
These distance classes were used for calculating the weighted heat risk layers:
≤ 5 000 m
5 000,1-15 000 m
15 000,1-30 000 m
30 000,1-50 000 m
> 50 000 m

Data sources:
Service points (edited to include hospitals and other healthcare facilities, duplicates and some irrelevant facilities removed) Lounaistieto. CC BY 4.0.

Heat risk in the Turku region between 23.5.-1.6.2024 for people aged 65 or over​ (edited classes with distance to nearest emergency care offering hospitals) Lounaistieto. Data licence and download site not yet published.
