About this map

This webmap visualises the distribution of forest reserves, and national and international conservation areas in Tanzania. International conservation areas consist of UNESCO World Heritage sites, as well as internationally important wetlands (Ramsar Sites).

Data layers: Protected planet.

UNEP-WCMC (2026). Protected Area Profile for United Republic of Tanzania from the World Database on Protected Areas, September 2026. Available at: www.protectedplanet.net
